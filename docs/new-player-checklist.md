# Super Sonic New Player Checklist

Complete this checklist before joining a full squadron mission for the first time. It is designed to catch configuration problems before mission night.

## Required software and content

- DCS World is installed and updated to the squadron's current version.
- The required terrain and aircraft modules are installed.
- The F-14 is available and launches correctly.
- Supercarrier is installed where required by the mission.
- DCS-SRS is installed and launches correctly.
- The current mission file or server details have been provided.

## Baseline gameplay settings

Open **Options > Gameplay** and confirm:

- **Easy Communication: Off**
- **Radio Assists: Off**
- **No force from mission, use mine: Off**
- **F10 Map User Marks: On**
- **Units: Imperial**
- **G-Effects: Simulation**

See [Super Sonic Baseline DCS Settings](baseline-dcs-settings.md) for explanations and optional settings.

## F-14 bindings

Confirm and test:

- Communications menu
- Tomcat Ball
- SRS push-to-talk
- Pilot radio push-to-talk controls
- Jester menu controls
- Wheel brakes and nosewheel steering
- Catapult salute or launch controls used by the current module version
- DLC and speed-brake controls
- Hook, flaps and wing-sweep controls

## Audio and SRS test

- The correct microphone is selected.
- The correct headphones or speakers are selected.
- SRS push-to-talk is not double-bound to an unwanted command.
- The SRS overlay appears in DCS.
- The player can transmit and receive on the agreed test frequency.
- Microphone level is clear without clipping or excessive background noise.

## Carrier hot-start validation

Use a simple test mission or the current base template:

1. Enter an F-14 hot start on the carrier.
2. Open the communications menu using the normal binding.
3. Confirm menu items can be selected with the mouse.
4. Press **Tomcat Ball** and confirm the voice call is heard.
5. Confirm carrier deck crew and catapult operation are available.
6. Confirm carrier TACAN and ICLS can be selected and received.
7. Confirm the F10 map opens and user marks are available.
8. Confirm SRS connects when using the squadron server.

## Multiplayer validation

Before the first full mission, join a short multiplayer test and confirm:

- The intended aircraft slot is available.
- Other players can hear and identify the new player on SRS.
- The F10 radio menu works.
- The aircraft can taxi, launch and leave the carrier safely.
- The player can rejoin or respawn without unexpected configuration problems.
- The player knows where the briefing, radio plan and kneeboard information are stored.

## Known F-14 menu issue

When **Easy Communication** is enabled, the F-14 communications menu may open but refuse mouse selection.

Preferred fix: turn Easy Communication off and restart DCS.

Immediate workaround: press the bound **Tomcat Ball** command, which may restore mouse interaction.

See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) for the controlled test and full procedure.

## Ready for mission night

A player is ready when they can:

- Join the server and occupy the correct slot.
- Communicate clearly through SRS.
- Open and operate the comms/F10 menu.
- Launch from the carrier.
- Navigate using the published radio and navigation plan.
- Recover or divert without requiring configuration changes during the mission.
